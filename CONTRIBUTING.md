# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in contributing. To keep reviews quick and the history easy to follow:

1. **Keep changes focused.** Make one logical change per branch and pull request, and describe what it does and why in the PR description.
2. **Check your work before opening a PR.** Run the project's tests and linters locally, and confirm that your change builds and behaves as described.
3. **Be respectful in discussions.** Give constructive feedback, assume good intent, and be open to review comments.
