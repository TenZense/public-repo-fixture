# Contributing

Thanks for taking the time to contribute. A few general guidelines:

1. **Keep changes focused.** Make each pull request address one clear purpose, and describe what changed and why in the PR description.
2. **Check your work before submitting.** Run the project's tests and linters where they exist, and confirm your change does what it claims.
3. **Be respectful in discussions.** Assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and be open to revising your approach based on review.
