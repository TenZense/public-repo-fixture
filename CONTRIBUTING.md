# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in contributing! To keep things smooth for everyone:

1. **Keep changes focused.** Make each pull request address one clear change, and describe what it does and why in the PR description.
2. **Check your work before submitting.** Run the project's tests and linters locally, and make sure your change builds cleanly.
3. **Be respectful in discussions.** Give constructive, specific feedback on issues and reviews, and assume good intent from others.
