# Contributing

Thanks for taking the time to contribute. A few guidelines help keep changes easy to review:

1. **Keep changes focused.** Make one logical change per pull request, and describe what it does and why in the PR description.
2. **Check your work before opening a pull request.** Read through your diff, fix any typos or broken links, and confirm the existing content still renders correctly.
3. **Be respectful in discussions.** Assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and be open to revising your changes based on review.
