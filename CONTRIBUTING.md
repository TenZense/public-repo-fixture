# Contributing

Thanks for taking the time to contribute. These guidelines keep changes easy to review.

1. **Open an issue first for larger changes.** Describe the problem and your proposed approach so maintainers can weigh in before you invest significant time.
2. **Keep pull requests small and focused.** One logical change per PR, with a clear description of what changed and why.
3. **Write clear commit messages and test your changes.** Use a short summary line, explain the reasoning in the body if needed, and confirm your change works before requesting review.
