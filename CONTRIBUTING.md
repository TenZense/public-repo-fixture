# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in contributing! To keep things smooth for everyone:

1. **Open an issue first for larger changes.** For anything beyond a small fix, describe the problem and proposed approach so maintainers can weigh in before you invest significant time.
2. **Keep changes focused.** Submit one logical change per pull request, with a clear description of what changed and why.
3. **Be respectful and constructive.** Review feedback is about the code, not the author. Assume good intent and be open to revising your work.
